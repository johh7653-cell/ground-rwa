## The standard

Keep historical observations dated. Label current market references with their provenance. Preserve unknowns instead of manufacturing estimates. Validate allocations before saving. Make the distinction between a research plan and an actual holding visible.

Execution remains a future integration, with explicit routes, units, fees, simulation, wallet approval and confirmation. No token supply, launch terms, burns or revenue claims are configured.
