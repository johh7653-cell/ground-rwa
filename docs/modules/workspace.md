GROUND’s website connects asset discovery, source reading and budget planning in one restrained black interface. The complete catalogue stays accessible alongside the Solana starting view.

## The visitor path

| Surface | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Budget-based references, category shelves, issuer links and historical liquidity ranking |
| Assets | Search, network/category/issuer/source/coverage filters, pagination and list/grid views |
| Asset details | Backing descriptions, saved addresses, source fields, market observations and available curves |
| Watchlist | Device-local save/remove, refresh persistence and cross-tab updates |
| Blueprint & Basket | Validated allocation weights, product selection and local plan saving |
| Compare & Discover | Exact archived sample comparisons and distinct product discovery |
| Workspace | Local plans, validated JSON import, export, copy and removal |
| Trade | Solana selection, amount, current indexed reference and indicative quantity |

Filters are shareable through the URL. Planning does not require a wallet. Trade remains a preview; no transaction is signed or submitted.
