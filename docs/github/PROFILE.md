# GROUND

### Your wallet. A real-world side.

A Solana-first workspace for tokenized real-world assets: understand the product, follow its sources and build a plan that belongs to you.

GROUND brings stocks, funds, metals, property, credit and more into one research library. The complete catalogue contains **1,936 assets**, **50 issuers** and **16 categories**. Solana is the starting point; the wider source archive stays available.

## What you can use now

- Explore every asset, issuer and source, with dated market records and available historical curves.
- Save a watchlist and share your catalogue filters.
- Build a category Blueprint or product Basket; export and restore validated local plans.
- Connect a compatible Solana wallet to its public account and read its SOL balance.
- Open Trade to inspect current indexed market references and indicative quantities.

## Project components

| Component | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Discovery, asset details, issuers, sources, tools and Trade preview |
| Catalogue | Full preserved records, provenance and local identity images |
| Market references | Network/base-token matching, timeouts, cache and clear unavailable states |
| Wallet access | Wallet Standard public accounts and read-only mainnet SOL balance |
| API | Working archive, market-reference and balance endpoints |
| Methods | Thesis, calculation rules, architecture and implementation roadmap |

## Current stage

The research workspace, read-only wallet integration and Trade preview are implemented. Buying remains disabled while the execution service is prepared. Current price references and historical observations are labelled separately.

GROUND is a project identity. Listed assets retain their own issuers and product structures. No GROUND contract address, token launch terms or original project's social channels are included in this profile.
